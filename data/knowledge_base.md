## 2. Services and Solution Shapes

The four main service categories Axial Foundry builds are:

- Premium Websites
- Web Apps and Portals
- Applied AI Systems
- Automation and Data

Eight typical solution shapes (real client-grounded examples):

1. AI customer support assistant — Brand-aligned, answers questions, routes conversations, and hands off cleanly. Often integrated with WhatsApp.
2. Internal knowledge assistant — Turns scattered documents into a searchable RAG layer for internal teams.
3. CRM-connected chatbot workflow — Conversational layer linked to CRM, WhatsApp, and existing workflows.
4. Custom business dashboard — Reporting surface for pipelines, exceptions, and operational metrics.
5. Premium company website — Modern, conversion-aware, authoritative web presence.
6. Workflow automation system — Removes repetitive admin and connects internal tools.
7. Client portal or web app — Focused apps for customers, partners, or internal teams.
8. Speech and transcription workflow — Transcription, diarization, and voice-enabled processing.

Broader capabilities:

- Applied AI: LLMs, RAG, prompt engineering, evaluation, instruction tuning, Speech AI (ASR, diarization, TTS), Urdu and low-resource language NLP, and computer vision.
- Software Engineering: Python (Django, Flask, NestJS), REST and GraphQL APIs, SQL and PostgreSQL, and broader backend systems.
- Web and Product Delivery: Business websites, web apps, portals, dashboards, UX planning, launch support, and design-to-code in React, Next.js, and TypeScript.
- Automation and Data: Web scraping, data pipelines, CRM integration, reporting, monitoring, workflow systems, and GIS and spatial systems (Mapbox, ArcGIS, Google Earth Engine).

Axial Foundry explicitly builds both AI-heavy and non-AI solutions. They build premium websites, web apps, and automation projects, and add AI selectively where it creates clear value. They support agencies as a technical delivery partner and can work inside existing stacks and products.
